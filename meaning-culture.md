# Meaning and culture

Use this page to develop a semiotic analysis. Instead of treating communication as message transfer only, show how signs, symbols, and conventions produce meaning in culture.

Connect your example to how people interpret cues differently depending on community norms, history, and media environment.

## Draft prompts

- Which signs are central to your case?
- How do different audiences decode those signs?
- What meanings become dominant or contested?
