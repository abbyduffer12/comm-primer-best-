# Interpretation and power

Use Stuart Hall's encoding/decoding framework to explain who has influence over meaning-making and why interpretations diverge.

You can identify preferred, negotiated, and oppositional readings to show how power and institutional position shape communication outcomes.

## Draft prompts

- Who encoded the message?
- What assumptions are built into the preferred reading?
- Which audiences resist or reinterpret the message, and why?
