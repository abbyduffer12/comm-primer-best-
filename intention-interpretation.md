# Interpretation and intention

Use Umberto Eco's framework to compare intentio auctoris (author's intention), intentio operis (text's intention), and intentio lectoris (reader's intention).

Then add **intentio algorithmi** to describe how algorithmic systems intervene in interpretation through ranking, recommendation, filtering, and synthetic generation.

## Draft prompts

- Where do author, text, reader, and algorithmic intentions align?
- Where do they conflict?
- What new misunderstandings appear when AI intermediates the exchange?
